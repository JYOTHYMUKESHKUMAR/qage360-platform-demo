About Q-AGE 360

A global platform for healthy ageing, functional longevity & geriatric rehabilitation

The world is ageing rapidly. Healthy ageing, geroscience, and geriatric rehabilitation are no longer optional—they define the future of healthcare.

Q-AGE 360 is an open platform dedicated to sharing expert knowledge, driving innovation, and promoting integrated care across borders.

Healthy ageing is the process of maintaining physical, mental and social wellbeing as we grow older — preventing disease, preserving independence and sustaining quality of life. At its core is functional longevity: extending not just lifespan, but the number of years lived with strength, mobility, cognitive clarity and the ability to perform everyday activities without assistance.

Q-AGE 360 places this focus at the centre of its programming — geroscience, rehabilitation, integrated and virtual care models, and evidence-based strategies that help older adults live longer, healthier and more functionally independent lives.

  • Brings together healthcare professionals, geriatric specialists and rehabilitation experts
  • Connects researchers, academics and international healthcare leaders
  • Facilitates global knowledge sharing, best-practice exchange and continuous professional development
  • Champions functional longevity — helping older adults stay mobile, independent and engaged for longer
